Key analytical cautions

Counts describe notified records, not incidence or prevalence. Comparing areas or demographic groups requires suitable population denominators.
Outcome percentages are unadjusted proportions of the selected records. Treatment cohorts, outcome follow-up periods, and exclusions should be verified before official reporting.
The 2020 monthly pattern should not be interpreted as evidence of COVID-19 effects without external diagnostic-service and historical data.
Source: provided workbook “SABAH-TUARAN-TUARAN-TBIS101A-2020.xlsx” (185 records). Dataset is de-identified in this HTML: patient names, dates of birth, street addresses and coordinates are excluded. Diagnostic labels are grouped analytically: pulmonary includes explicit PTB / pulmonary / MDRTB labels; other TB sites are classified as extrapulmonary. “MDRTB” is not sufficient on its own to determine anatomical site and is grouped under pulmonary provisionally; verify against the source record.
